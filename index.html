Who We Are

About Us

The University of Regina Campus Conservatives bring together students who believe in free expression, responsible government, and meaningful civic engagement.

We provide a welcoming space for open discussion, networking, and active involvement in conservative politics — on campus and beyond. Whether you're deeply involved in politics or just curious, there's a place for you here.

Events & Speakers

We host discussions, guest speakers, debates, and social events throughout the academic year — giving you direct access to ideas that matter.
